# Dot Watch agent guidance

## Public identity privacy

This is a public repository. Publish under `stonethunk`, use the approved Git identity
`cognomen` with `sysop@stonethunk.sh`, and use the neutral `dev.dotwatch.app` identifier namespace. Do not publish the owner's
personal name, email, other identifying aliases, home directory paths, device
or account identifiers, signing team identifiers, or identifying image metadata
without explicit authorization for those specific details and this surface.
Building, committing, pushing, or publishing does not grant that authorization.
Existing disclosure does not grant permission to repeat it.

Keep real signing settings and credentials in ignored local configuration.
Use synthetic examples and preserve third-party license notices. Before any
public push, inspect the files, assets, author and committer identities, commit
and tag messages, and all reachable history. Check screenshots visually.

Preserve the existing tests and verification tools when changing app identifiers.

`cognomen` is the chief identity. `stonethunk` is the public organization,
`stonethunk.sh` is its domain, and `sysop@stonethunk.sh` is its contact address.
Before public pushes or deployments, verify the actual authenticated identity
and destination as well as the commit metadata. Do not silently fall back to
personal or corporate account credentials.
