# Private Watch audio test

This is a hardware/codec checkpoint for Watch voice. It records at most five seconds locally, stops capture, converts the sample to Opus and back, then plays the decoded audio through the Watch's selected output. It does not connect to Dot or any server, use credentials, transfer a recording, or save an audio file. Launching the app or tapping the complication never starts this test.

1. End any phone voice session first. Open **Dot** on the Watch and scroll down to **Test Watch audio**.
2. Tap the button and allow microphone access. Keep Dot visible and your wrist raised through the five-second countdown and playback (about ten seconds total); speak during recording, then listen for your sample.
3. **Stop audio test** stops capture/playback and discards the sample. Leaving the view, dimming, interruption, output removal or cancellation also stops the test. A failed audio-session release leaves Stop available for retry.

The permission sheet may briefly deactivate the view; permission itself is allowed to finish, but capture waits until the view is active. A late system activation callback is cleaned up before another test can start. The complication and public Watch configuration have no microphone permission or diagnostic controls.

The sample stays in bounded memory. `BoundedCaptureBuffer` accepts mono/stereo float PCM at 8–96 kHz, caps it at five seconds, and rejects late callbacks after close/take. Playback is mono 48 kHz PCM after native Opus conversion. The test uses voice processing, the system audio route and asynchronous Watch audio-session activation. It releases the engine, playback buffers and audio session on every exit. No audio, token, account identifier or conversation content is logged.

`Documents/audio-diagnostics.json` records fixed status labels, boolean hardware milestones, frame/packet counts and a signal-present boolean. Schema 2 adds start/update times and a fixed cancellation reason (user Stop, dimmed screen, inactive app, closed view, interruption, disconnected output or media reset). It checkpoints before audio startup and at each stage so a new crash can be localized. It cannot prove that a person heard the output; that confirmation remains required. Do not mark Dot voice, locked-phone/wrist-down voice, network continuity or battery suitability complete from this local test.
