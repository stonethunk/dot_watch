## Worth considering, with current restrictions

- [Codex Ambassadors](https://developers.openai.com/community/codex-ambassadors): **applications paused**. Intended for community organizers, maintainers, teachers and power users supporting other builders. Published commitment is about 2–4 hours weekly and one contribution monthly; support includes credits, starter kits, team/community contact and events. Voluntary and unpaid. A plausible future fit if you want to teach or organize local workshops; it is not an available application today.
- [OpenAI Grove](https://openai.com/index/openai-grove/): earliest-stage technical founders, mentoring, peers and research-team exposure. The published January 2026 cohort's applications **closed January 12**; its dates are past. No currently open future cohort was verified. Useful later if turning this into a company.
- [DevDay Exchanges](https://events.openai.com/devdayexchange2026): applications are being reviewed and the official page still welcomes applications. Dates: Bengaluru October 16; Tokyo October 20; Seoul October 22; Berlin October 26; Paris October 28; London November 3; São Paulo November 6; Mexico City November 10. These are events, not standing membership. The September 29 San Francisco DevDay is past and its applications are closed. Check acceptance and travel details before making plans.
- [Codex for Students](https://developers.openai.com/community/students): conditional on current verified enrollment and residence in the US or Canada. Offers $100 in ChatGPT credits for Codex, not API credits. Student status is not established for either tester group member.

## Suggested order

Join Academy and the Startup Community if their activities interest you. Consider the OSS program using a genuinely qualifying public project. Keep the private Dot iteration moving; once its physical demo is reliable, use it to support the already prepared Sign in with ChatGPT/Dot access request. Ambassadors and Grove are future-cohort possibilities rather than current enrollment options.

This order is an assessment of fit, not an assurance of admission, credits, early access, endorsement, or Dot integration rights. Recheck the linked official pages before applying; several search results describe previous cohorts or already-past events as upcoming.
